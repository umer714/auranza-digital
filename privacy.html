Information you provide

If you contact AURANZA through the enquiry form, the details you enter are placed into an email draft on your own device. The website does not submit that form to a server. If you send the email, we may use the information in it to respond to your enquiry and discuss possible services.

Website technologies

This static website may load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google's servers to retrieve those font files. No analytics, advertising pixels, or account-registration system are intentionally included in this starter version.

How information may be used

Information you choose to send may be used to respond, communicate about a project, and maintain relevant business records. We do not claim to sell personal information. Do not send sensitive personal information through a general enquiry.

Retention and security

Messages may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential engagement, and meet applicable obligations. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent, contact auranza.digital@gmail.com. Applicable rights depend on your location and circumstances.

Updates

This notice may be updated as the website or business practices change. Replace this starter text with a legally reviewed notice that accurately describes your actual practices before collecting enquiries at scale.
